Employee Attrition Prediction System

This Machine Learning application predicts whether an employee is likely to leave an organization based on employee-related attributes such as age, salary, job satisfaction, work hours, experience, and department.

Project Objective

To help organizations identify employees who may be at risk of leaving the company and take proactive retention measures.

Algorithm Used

Random Forest Classification is used to classify whether an employee may leave or stay in the organization.

Input Features

Age, Salary, Job Satisfaction, Work Hours, Experience, and Department.

Prediction Output

The model predicts either: Employee May Stay or Employee May Leave.
