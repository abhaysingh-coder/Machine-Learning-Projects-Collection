Car Price Prediction Model

This model predicts the estimated selling price of a car using Machine Learning based on car age, kilometers driven, fuel type, owner type, mileage, and engine size.

Input Features

Car Age, KM Driven, Fuel Type, Owner Type, Mileage, and Engine Size.

Target Output

The model predicts the estimated car price in Indian Rupees.

Algorithm Used

Simple Linear Regression is used to learn the relationship between car features and price.

Technology Stack

Python, Django, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, Joblib, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
