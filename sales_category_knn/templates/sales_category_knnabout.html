Sales Category Prediction Using KNN

This Machine Learning application predicts sales categories using the K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) algorithm. It helps businesses analyze sales performance and classify products into different sales categories based on sales metrics.

Project Objective

To classify sales records into meaningful categories and assist businesses in making data-driven decisions.

Algorithm Used

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) classifies sales records based on similarity with existing sales data.

Input Features

Sales-related attributes such as revenue, quantity sold, profit and customer activity.

Prediction Output

Predicts the sales category of a product or sales record.

Dataset Information

Problem Type

Classification

Target

Sales Category

Algorithm

KNN

Output

Category Label

Key Evaluation Factors

Sales Volume

Total quantity of products sold.

Revenue

Total earnings generated from sales.

Profit

Net profit generated by products.

Customer engagement and purchasing behavior.

Historical sales performance analysis.
