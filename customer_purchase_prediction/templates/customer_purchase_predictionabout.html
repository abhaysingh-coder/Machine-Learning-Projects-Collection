Customer Purchase Prediction System

A Machine Learning application that predicts whether a customer is likely to purchase a product based on Age and Estimated Salary.

Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer behavior and predict purchasing decisions using historical customer data.

Machine Learning Algorithm

Logistic Regression is used as the classification algorithm to determine whether a customer will purchase a product.

Input Features

Age and Estimated Salary are used as input features for customer purchase prediction.

Prediction Output

The model predicts either: Will Purchase or Will Not Purchase.
